# Handwritten research-use undertaking / 手写研究用途承诺书

Choose either language below. Copy the undertaking text **entirely by hand** on
blank paper, fill in your details, sign, and date it. Email a clear scan or photo
to **meidachuan@tju.edu.cn**. Do not upload the completed document to GitHub.

以下中文与英文版本任选其一。请在空白纸上**亲笔抄写承诺书正文**，填写申请信息、
亲笔签名并注明日期，然后将清晰扫描件或照片发送至 **meidachuan@tju.edu.cn**。

## 中文正文

**ENUSP 源代码研究用途承诺书**

本人申请获取 ENUSP（Explicit Decoupling of Environment, User, and Position for
Generalizable Gesture Recognition）项目源代码，并郑重承诺：

一、所获代码仅用于非商业性科学研究、方法学习、实验复现及学术评测。

二、未经作者另行书面许可，不将代码用于商业产品、收费服务或商业部署。

三、未经作者书面许可，不公开、上传、转发或再分发所获源代码，以及包含该代码的修改版本。
其他研究者需要代码时，应自行向作者申请。

四、使用该代码形成论文或其他研究成果时，引用 ENUSP 论文，保留作者归属信息，
并如实说明所作修改及实验设置。

五、自行向原始数据集提供方获取数据，遵守相关数据与第三方软件的使用条件。

## English text

**Undertaking for research use of the ENUSP source code**

I request the source code of ENUSP (Explicit Decoupling of Environment, User,
and Position for Generalizable Gesture Recognition) and undertake that:

1. I will use the supplied code only for non-commercial scientific research,
   method study, reproducibility work, and academic evaluation.
2. I will not use the code in commercial products, paid services, or commercial
   deployment without separate written permission from the authors.
3. I will not publish, upload, forward, or redistribute the supplied code or a
   modified version containing it without the authors' written permission.
   Other researchers requiring access will submit their own applications.
4. I will cite the ENUSP paper in resulting publications, retain authorship and
   attribution notices, and accurately describe my modifications and experiments.
5. I will obtain data from its original provider and follow the applicable
   dataset and third-party software conditions.

I have read and understood this undertaking. The application information below
is accurate, and I agree to follow these conditions.
